Accessibility

Last updated: 5 November 2026

We want everyone to enjoy Pip & Flicker: the game and this website. This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What this website does

  • Everything works with a keyboard alone, and you can always see where you are.
  • It works with screen readers such as VoiceOver. Every picture that matters has a description.
  • If your device asks for less motion, nothing moves by itself: no video starts on its own and the effects stand still. Every video has a pause button, and the fireflies at the top can be paused too.
  • Text has strong contrast, also on top of pictures.
  • You can zoom to 200 %, or use a screen only 320 pixels wide, without scrolling sideways.
  • It works with high-contrast colour settings, such as Windows Contrast themes.
  • It uses no cookies, no tracking and nothing from other companies' servers.

The accessibility button

At the bottom left of every page there is a round button with the accessibility figure. It opens a small panel where you can choose:

  • Text size: 100 %, 125 %, 150 % or 200 %.
  • Stronger contrast: brighter text, firmer borders and solid backgrounds behind text on pictures.
  • Stop all motion: the videos and the fireflies stand still.
  • Underline all links.
  • Easier-to-read text: your device's own clear font, with more space between lines, letters and words.

Your choices stay on this device and are never sent anywhere. Reset puts everything back. The panel works with a keyboard too: Esc closes it.

In the game

The game has its own accessibility settings: VoiceOver, Audio Guide, High Visibility, Text size, Reduce Motion and Haptics. See what each one does.

Known limits

  • The short gameplay clips have no sound. The text next to each clip says what it shows.
  • The accessibility button needs JavaScript. Without it, the pages still work, and your browser's own zoom and text settings still help.

How we checked

On 5 November 2026 we checked every page again: with a keyboard only, with automatic checks of headings, labels, links and colour contrast, at 200 % text size and 320 pixels wide, with extra space between lines, letters and words, with reduced motion and with forced colours. We also tried every setting of the accessibility button. On 23 October 2026 we measured colour contrast against the real pictures.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on this website or in the game is hard to use, please write to support@zmeustudio.com. Tell us what you tried and which device you use. We read every message.

Forest tricksters

Some forest folk love a prank.

Not everyone in the woods wants to be friends. Learn their little tricks, and Pip runs right past.

  • Sham-Pignon

    By day

    A mushroom in disguise. Real fruit keeps its colour, but this one blinks. Leave it be!

  • The Creeper

    All day

    A vine that breathes at the forest's edge. If it grabs Pip, swipe to wriggle free before your chain drains away.

  • The Drip

    Dusk & night

    Wobbles on a branch above the path. When it stretches, hop to another path, or get splashed blue!

  • The Lure

    Dusk & night

    Glows like a treasure, but wanders across all three paths. Real fruit stays put. Don't touch!

  • Shadow Fox

    Dusk & night

    Waits low, then dashes straight at Pip. She never turns, so just step out of her way.

  • Moon Beetle

    Dusk & night

    Big, blue and not going anywhere. Simply go around!

  • Night Sprite

    Dusk & night

    Rushes at Pip like a trickster, but it's really a gift! Run right into it for five Seeds.

Bump into the fox or the beetle and the run is over, unless the friend running beside Pip takes the bump for her.

Accessibility

Everyone can play.

Play by eye, by ear or by touch. You'll find all of it in the game's Settings.

  • Audio Guide

    Play by ear. Sounds tell you what is coming on each path: the left path in your left ear, the right path in your right ear, the middle path in both. A rising sound means jump, a falling one means slide.

    • Pace: Medium or Hard
    • Guide sounds volume
    • Learn the sounds

    Always on while VoiceOver is running.

    • Left ear
    • Both ears
    • Right ear

  • VoiceOver

    Every screen and every button is read out. In a run, VoiceOver steps aside, so your swipes work just as usual.

  • High Visibility

    Big, clear signs right above Pip, one for each path: jump, slide, path closed, a creature or poison fruit. You choose their size and colour.

  • Text size

    Make every word bigger, up to 150 %. It follows your iPhone's text size, too.

  • Reduce Motion

    No camera shake, no swaying and no colour flash in a BLOOMBURST. It follows your iPhone's setting, too.

  • Haptics

    Every moment has its own feel: a feather-light tap when you gather, a two-step thump when Pip lands. Switch it off any time.

  • Headphones on

    Best with headphones, at a low, comfortable volume. With the Audio Guide, each side path is in one ear.

    Tip for parents: on iPhone, Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Headphone Safety › Reduce Loud Sounds keeps headphone volume gentle. You can lock it with Screen Time.

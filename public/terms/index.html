5. Health and safety

Flashing lights. Pip & Flicker has lightning flashes during storms and a bright burst of light when a BLOOMBURST happens. A very small number of people can have seizures or blackouts when they see flashing lights or patterns, even if this has never happened to them before. If you, or anyone in your family, have ever had a seizure or epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing. Stop playing straight away and see a doctor if you feel dizzy or sick, or have twitching eyes or muscles, blurred or changed vision, confusion, or any movement you cannot control. Grown-ups: please keep an eye on young children while they play.

Take breaks. Play in a well-lit room, hold the phone at a comfortable distance, and take a 10–15 minute break every hour. Stop if your eyes, hands or wrists get tired or sore.

Play somewhere safe. Do not play while walking, cycling, crossing a road, driving, or anywhere you need to watch what is around you. Keep the volume comfortable, especially with headphones.

This section is advice to keep you safe. It does not reduce our responsibility under section 6.
